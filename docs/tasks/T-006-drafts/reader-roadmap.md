# What you can read, and what's coming

## Available now

**《AI 写完了，能跑，然后呢？》 / *AI Wrote It. It Runs. Then What?***

- **Chinese:** the complete reading order of 51 pieces on the [Chinese site](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/zh/read/), and a [209-page A5 PDF](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/downloads/volume-one-zh-a5.pdf).
- **English:** the preface and the browser chapter in full, plus introductions to two more chapters that link to the Chinese originals, on the [English site](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/read/).
- **Reading tools:** search, sharing a passage, adjusting text size, local bookmarks, and returning to where you left off on the same device.

## Next

- A revised edition of the first book. It opens more directly, adds a short introduction and a map to each part, and gives the key chapters deeper explanations with worked examples. The voice stays conversational.
- More chapters in English, starting with the first chapter and the AI-evaluation chapter.
- A calmer reader: one accent colour, a choice of paper tones, serif text, figures you can open and close without losing your place, and comfortable text on phones.
- The first chapter of 《活交给 AI，我去钓会儿鱼》, on how this book was made.

## Later

- 《省下来的时间，去哪儿了？》 as a serial on this site.
- A new PDF of the revised edition. The current 209-page one stays available until then.
- EPUB editions.
