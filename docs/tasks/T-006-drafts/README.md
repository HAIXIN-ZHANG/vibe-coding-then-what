# Three books on AI, from the code to the rest of the day

AI now does the work, and it runs. But the person who asked often can't say what it actually did. These books follow that gap from three directions, each in the same light, story-like voice.

### AI Wrote It. It Runs. Then What? · 《AI 写完了，能跑，然后呢？》

*Stories from the system behind the code you didn't write*

A course website says "You're enrolled." The button worked. Is your name on the list? Follow one sign-up through the page, the API, the database, the background jobs, the release and the bad morning after, and finally into an AI assistant that answers students' questions. It is for anyone who can already build something with AI but can't yet explain why it works, or why it breaks.

**Read it now.** The [Chinese edition](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/zh/read/) has all 51 pieces, from the preface to the closing, and a [209-page A5 PDF](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/downloads/volume-one-zh-a5.pdf). In [English](https://haixin-zhang.github.io/vibe-coding-then-what/read/) you can read the preface and the browser chapter in full, plus introductions to two more chapters that link to the Chinese originals.

We are revising selected chapters for deeper technical explanation while keeping the voice.

### Leave It to the Agents. I'm Going Fishing. · 《活交给 AI，我去钓会儿鱼》

*How one book went from vibe coding to agentic coding*

One person, a few AIs, one book. How long did it take before the author dared hand over the work and walk away? This short book follows the real project in this repository: one chat window at first, then role-playing agents and their pile of paperwork, then two different AIs checking each other. The rework is included.

**In development.**

### Where Does the Saved Time Go? · 《省下来的时间，去哪儿了？》

*Small talk on living with AI*

AI wrote the email in a minute. Who actually reads it, and what happens to the half hour you saved? Conversational pieces on work, home and a free afternoon, each with a small habit to take away. No programming needed.

**Coming as a serial** on this site.

## About the author

The books are written under the pen name **缸子太子与世界 (Jar & Prince Explore the World)**, after the author's two cats. **Jason Zhang** maintains the project and is open to engineering opportunities and collaboration; find him on [GitHub](https://github.com/HAIXIN-ZHANG). AI helped with research, drafting and revision throughout, as the [preface](book/revisions/zh/volume-one-making-of/00-before-we-start.md) acknowledges. The choices, checks and remaining mistakes are the author's.
