# PDR-001 · First-volume release scope

Status: author-confirmed direction; open release conditions remain. Decision-maker: author. Confirmed/reconstructed: 26 November 2026; earlier two-book planning remains in its dated sources. Recorded: 26 November 2026. Relevant planning PR: #47 (not an implementation release).

## Context and choice

The engineering book has a complete comparison edition but new author feedback asks for technical depth and a more comfortable reader. The life book and production guide serve different purposes. Prioritise a useful, presentable first volume and its reading paths without making either later manuscript a prerequisite.

An alternative would couple all books to one launch. This comparison explains the boundary; no claim is made that a formal options workshop occurred. Independence keeps the first reader outcome reviewable while preserving later books' own schedules.

## Consequences and limits

The public label “Beta 1” is a candidate. It does not rename historical Milestone 1/Beta 1. The M4 amendment below resolves the language and PDF direction for this round; device/acceptance scope remains open in the PRD. Existing Chinese/English availability and the 209-page PDF stay honestly identified. A new planning label does not imply an unpublished revision is live.

## Amendment · M4 reference samples and paired designs

Author-confirmed on 26 November 2026, after the requirements batch was merged in PR #49; recorded against main `9742626`. The author accepted the next-step plan: assess all eight parts, deepen weak explanations and connections, and preserve material that already works. Use the two calibrated product-design and database chapters to establish the standard before assigning further manuscript batches.

This first batch covers those two samples, necessary teaching diagrams/tables, database-example verification within available facilities, and paired EN/ZH reader designs. The reason for this sequence is to check depth and reading comfort on concrete material before repeating the approach. It is not a measured productivity result. Scope confirmation is separate from manuscript acceptance, database execution, design acceptance and publication.
