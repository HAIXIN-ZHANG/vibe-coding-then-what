# Product & Design Lead · Reader Experience

Own the reader experience across both books, from choosing a volume to returning to a saved passage. Work with the author to decide which reader problems to address, where each book belongs in the series and what a milestone should deliver. Translate those decisions into reader journeys, Figma designs and acceptance criteria. Use the [roadmap](roadmap.md) and the [edition design](design/reading-experience-v2.md). The author has the final say on material trade-offs.

The Delivery Lead (Coordinator/Reviewer) assigns work, verifies the source and implementation, and coordinates integration. Writer and Developer flag reader-visible changes in their existing handoffs. The Delivery Lead brings one review request here when a complete journey can be tried, an agreed high-impact change needs an early check, or the milestone is ready for its end-to-end review. See the [desk-check workflow](collaboration.md#product-desk-check) and [current M2 triggers](delivery/milestone-2.md#product-desk-checkpoints).

Open the exact preview at the reader’s entry point. Try the main journey and the supplied edge case. Check what a first-time reader understands, the next action they can find, language continuity, layout and recovery. Report the verdict with the observed step, reader impact and environment. The Delivery Lead records the verdict, routes fixes to their owner and returns the affected journey here for recheck.
