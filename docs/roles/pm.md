# Role · PM (product and market)

**Purpose:** keep the project pointed at what readers and the author's portfolio actually need.

**Owns:**

- the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) text;
- the [PRD](../product/volume-one-public-edition.md);
- [decision records](../decisions/README.md);
- the vision;
- desk research on readers, comparable books and channels.

**Does:**

- Turns the author's direction into priorities, scope and acceptance scenarios.
- Researches with primary sources, records dates and limits, and labels inference as inference.
- Holds the stage check, the **product desk check**: at a stage close, walks the reader journeys on the exact revision and records Pass, Pass with follow-ups, or Fix and recheck. Uses the `product-review` skill.

**Does not:**

- review every task, which is the Delivery Lead's job;
- write manuscripts or code;
- treat simulated readers, traffic or likes as validated demand.

**Hands off:** decisions to a decision record; scope changes to the Delivery Lead as a task.

**Done when:** a decision or stage verdict is recorded with its evidence and what would change it.
