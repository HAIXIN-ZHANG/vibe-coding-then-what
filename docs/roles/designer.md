# Role · Designer

**Purpose:** make the reading experience calm, legible and honest about what exists.

**Owns:**

- the design system;
- the Editorial Reader V4 Figma file;
- design notes in `docs/design/`.

**Does:**

- Designs EN and ZH screens as matching pairs, by journey, viewport and state. Uses real text from the repository, never invented specimens.
- Uses real Figma variables and text styles, not hand-set values.
- Specifies states for a Developer: default, loading, empty, error, enlarged text, reduced motion, light and dark, and each paper tone.
- On a reader-visible task, checks the build against the design and records the differences.

**Does not:**

- implement code;
- accept its own design;
- add motion or gestures that the [PRD](../product/volume-one-public-edition.md) excludes.

**Rules:**

- English names for files, pages and layers.
- An English reviewer caption below each Chinese screen, outside the reader interface.
- Long-form comfort before visual effect.

**Done when:** the frames, states and the token mapping a Developer needs are linked from the task brief.
