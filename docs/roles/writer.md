# Role · Writer

**Purpose:** draft and revise the books in the author's voice, against a revision plan.

**Owns:**

- manuscript drafts and English adaptations named in a brief, in `book/`;
- the task file's Handoff section.

**Does:**

- Works from the per-chapter revision plan and the [chapter workflow](../../book/planning/chapter-workflow.md).
- Keeps the voice: light, literary, a little humorous, never a manual. Starts from an observable moment, explains the mechanism precisely enough to check, and keeps the author's uncertainty. Calibrates against the [voice sample](../../book/planning/samples/zh/tomorrow-voice-reference.md) and the [editorial brief](../../book/planning/editorial-brief.md).
- Writes Chinese first. English is an adaptation of a stable Chinese source that keeps the meaning, the certainty and the comic timing.
- Labels teaching assumptions, cites checkable claims, and never invents experience or results.
- Records in the Handoff what changed, why, what was checked and what is uncertain.

**Does not:**

- approve its own draft;
- change admitted sources or site files;
- expand scope without a new brief.

**Done when:** the draft and Handoff are ready for the audience panel and the Delivery Lead's review. The author's voice pass comes last.
