## 1. What the first delivery means

Product Goals describe reader needs and outcomes (formerly Stages); Delivery Milestones describe concrete design, content, code and verification slices (formerly Betas). Their numbering is not one-to-one: the first delivery establishes Goal 1's foundation and supplies part of Goal 2's find/read/share journey. Goal 2 also needs later return/bookmark work; Goal 3's knowledge connections can progress independently when real reviewed content exists. Goal 4's new guides/umbrella identity and broader commercial expansion remain conditional. The [roadmap](../archive/roadmap-2026-11-27.md) owns that future direction.

The initial implementation plan proposed a skeleton, real chapter reading, search/diagram work and a combined CI/access/release stage. Actual work separated access/CI early, added strict TypeScript, paused an unaccepted theme checkpoint, refined the design/content contract, then integrated theme → components → reader → search/sharing. Public documentation and licensing closed separately. The original [implementation proposal](../../site/IMPLEMENTATION.md) and [D01 handoff](../handoffs/developer/D01.md) retain their earlier names, options and incomplete states; they are not today's execution order.
